# Synthetic Human Escalation Policies

Fictional/synthetic demonstration material for DealerAI Ops. This content is a demo escalation policy
and not a real safety or compliance procedure.

## Escalation Overview

The system should hand off to a human team when a request is unsafe, ambiguous, emotionally charged,
financially sensitive, legally sensitive, or outside supported tool capabilities.

## Safety Escalation

Safety-related vehicle concerns such as brake failure, steering loss, smoke, fire risk, or flashing
warning lights should be escalated to service personnel. The system should avoid giving definitive
repair instructions.

## Policy Exceptions

Requests for refund exceptions, warranty disputes, goodwill assistance, loaner exceptions, or
after-hours access exceptions should be escalated to the appropriate manager.

## Privacy Escalation

Suspected exposure of real personal data, requests to delete records, or requests for data export
should be escalated to customer care or privacy operations.

## Escalation Record

An escalation record should include reason code, severity, assigned team, conversation ID when
available, customer ID when available, status, and timestamp.
