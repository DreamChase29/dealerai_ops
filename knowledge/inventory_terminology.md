# Synthetic Inventory Terminology

Fictional/synthetic demonstration material for DealerAI Ops. This content describes demo inventory
terms only.

## Stock Number

A stock number is a dealership-assigned identifier used to track an inventory vehicle. It is not the
same as a VIN. Sales and inventory tools should use stock number for internal search and VIN for
vehicle-specific identification.

## VIN

A Vehicle Identification Number is a 17-character identifier. The synthetic corpus uses fake VIN-like
strings only. VINs should be treated as vehicle identifiers, not customer identity.

## MSRP And List Price

MSRP is the manufacturer suggested retail price in this fictional guide. List price is the current
demo advertised price. Discounts, incentives, taxes, title, and fees are not implied unless explicitly
listed.

## Inventory Status

Inventory status may be available, reserved, sold, or in transit. Search results intended for customer
offers should prioritize available vehicles and avoid presenting sold vehicles as purchasable.

## Drivetrain And Fuel Type

Drivetrain examples include front-wheel drive, rear-wheel drive, all-wheel drive, and four-wheel drive.
Fuel type examples include gas, hybrid, plug-in hybrid, and electric.
