# Synthetic Sales Process FAQs

Fictional/synthetic demonstration material for DealerAI Ops. This content does not represent real
financing, pricing, or sales policy.

## Creating A Sales Lead

A sales lead captures customer interest in a vehicle or vehicle category. The synthetic lead record
may include source, desired make, desired model, budget range, customer ID, and inventory vehicle ID.
Creating a lead requires explicit confirmation.

## Test Drive Requests

Test drive requests should collect preferred time, desired vehicle, contact method, and license
eligibility indicator in a real deployment. This demo does not store real license numbers.

## Trade-In Questions

Trade-in estimates are not final offers. Customers should be told that vehicle condition, mileage,
history, and inspection affect final trade-in value.

## Pricing Questions

Inventory list price is not a final out-the-door amount. Taxes, registration, title, dealer fees, and
eligible incentives may change the final synthetic purchase estimate.

## Sold Or Reserved Vehicles

If a vehicle is sold or reserved, the system should offer similar available inventory instead of
claiming the vehicle can be purchased.
