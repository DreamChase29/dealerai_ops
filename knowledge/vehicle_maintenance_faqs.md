# Synthetic Vehicle Maintenance FAQs

Fictional/synthetic demonstration material for DealerAI Ops. This content is not a substitute for
vehicle-specific owner manuals.

## Check Engine Light

A steady check engine light means the vehicle should be diagnosed soon. A flashing check engine light
may indicate a severe misfire in this fictional guide, and the customer should avoid driving until a
qualified technician advises otherwise.

## Tire Pressure

Tire pressure should be checked when tires are cold. The correct pressure is usually listed on a
vehicle door placard, not necessarily on the tire sidewall. Seasonal temperature changes can affect
pressure.

## Wiper Blades

Wiper blades should be replaced when they streak, chatter, split, or fail to clear the windshield.
Many synthetic service visits include a visual wiper inspection.

## Alignment Symptoms

Pulling, uneven tire wear, an off-center steering wheel, or vibration can indicate alignment or tire
balance concerns. Alignment checks are commonly paired with tire replacement or suspension work.

## Electric Vehicle Maintenance

Electric vehicles still require tire rotations, brake inspections, cabin filters, software checks, and
coolant inspections. They do not require engine oil changes.
