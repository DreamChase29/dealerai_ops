# Synthetic Maintenance Policies

Fictional/synthetic demonstration material for DealerAI Ops. This content is not real dealership
policy and must not replace an owner manual or manufacturer guidance.

## Oil Change Intervals

Most synthetic gasoline vehicles use an oil change interval of 5,000 to 7,500 miles depending on
driving conditions. Severe-duty usage, frequent short trips, towing, and dusty roads may require
shorter intervals. Electric vehicles do not require engine oil changes.

## Tire Rotation

Tire rotation is recommended every 5,000 to 7,500 miles for most synthetic vehicles. Uneven tire wear,
vibration, or frequent highway driving may justify earlier inspection. Rotation patterns vary by
drivetrain and tire type.

## Brake Inspection

Brake inspections are recommended at least once per year or when customers report squealing, grinding,
pulsation, or increased stopping distance. Brake pad measurements should be documented in millimeters
on synthetic service history.

## Fluid Checks

Coolant, brake fluid, transmission fluid, washer fluid, and differential fluid should be inspected
during multipoint inspections. Fluid exchange recommendations depend on model, mileage, age, and
driving conditions.

## Battery Testing

Battery testing is recommended before seasonal temperature changes or when the vehicle starts slowly.
Hybrid and electric vehicle high-voltage battery concerns should be escalated to certified service
technicians.
