# Synthetic Warranty FAQs

Fictional/synthetic demonstration material for DealerAI Ops. This content is not a real warranty,
manufacturer statement, or legal representation.

## Basic Warranty

The demo basic warranty example covers synthetic defects in materials or workmanship for a fictional
time and mileage period. Warranty eligibility depends on vehicle record, in-service date, mileage, and
coverage type.

## Powertrain Warranty

The synthetic powertrain warranty example may include engine, transmission, and drivetrain components.
Wear items, accident damage, abuse, and maintenance neglect are excluded in this fictional example.

## Warranty Diagnosis Fees

Diagnostic fees may apply when a concern is not covered by warranty. If a covered warrantable failure
is confirmed, the diagnostic charge may be waived or billed to the fictional warranty provider.

## Maintenance And Warranty

Routine maintenance is usually the customer's responsibility unless a synthetic prepaid maintenance
plan applies. Skipping required maintenance may affect warranty coverage for related failures.

## Recalls

Recall checks should be performed using the vehicle VIN. Recall remedies are fictional in this demo
and should be scheduled with appropriate parts and certified technician availability.
