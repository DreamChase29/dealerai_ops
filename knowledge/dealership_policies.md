# Synthetic Dealership Policies

Fictional/synthetic demonstration material for DealerAI Ops. This content is not real dealership
policy and must not be used as legal, financial, or operational advice.

## Store Hours And Contact

Demo Auto Group sales is open Monday through Saturday from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM. The synthetic service
department is open Monday through Saturday from 7:30 AM to 6:00 PM. The dealership is closed on
Sundays and fictional regional holidays. Customers may contact the demo store by phone, web chat, or
email.

## Appointment Arrival Window

Service customers should arrive no earlier than 15 minutes before a scheduled appointment. Arrivals
more than 20 minutes late may be converted to standby status if the service lane is full. Advisors may
offer a later slot when capacity is available.

## Loaner Vehicle Policy

Loaner vehicles are fictional and limited to eligible warranty repairs expected to take more than four
business hours. A synthetic customer must present a valid license indicator in the demo system and sign
a loaner agreement placeholder. Loaners are not guaranteed for routine maintenance.

## Payment Policy

The demo dealership accepts synthetic credit card, debit card, ACH, and service-contract billing
records. Payment is due when repair work is completed unless a fictional warranty or service contract
authorization applies.

## Estimate Approval

Service advisors must obtain customer approval before adding paid repair work beyond the original
estimate. Digital approvals are recorded as synthetic consent events. An advisor may not represent an
unapproved estimate as final.

## After-Hours Drop-Off

After-hours drop-off is available through a secure key envelope process in this fictional scenario.
Customers should include vehicle, contact, concern, and appointment information on the envelope.
